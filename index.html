3D TABLE TENNIS

Pick a difficulty to start

  • Move the mouse — the paddle follows your cursor. Meet the ball to return it.
  • Flick up as you hit — topspin: the ball dips, so you can hit harder.
  • Flick down — backspin: slow floating chop that checks on the bounce.
  • Flick sideways — sidespin: the ball curves in the air.
  • Hit off-center to aim: left/right of the paddle angles the shot, high/low lofts it.
  • Click or Space to toss your serve; the paddle hits it on the way down.
